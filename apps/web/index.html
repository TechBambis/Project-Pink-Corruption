Session patterns & limits

Vibration 0% · Rotation 0%

Your limits apply to every command. Pause stops both channels; conversation cancels queued cues and keeps the current phase pattern. In event mode, the event haptics option controls cues and phase patterns are suspended.

Speech cues & timing calibration

Comparison needs enabled control, an unpaused session and speech cues enabled. Intensity is 0–1 and respects your vibration limit. If movement arrives after the word, increase that motor's lead by the measured delay. A lead cannot precede an already started cue. Impulses return to the ongoing pattern. These values affect playback; they do not measure physical movement.
