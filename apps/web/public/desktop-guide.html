Bambi Local · Linux packaging smoke

This build includes offline playback. AI, speech, microphone recognition, devices, online media and analysis are unavailable.

Load the offline chime sample, then press Start. Use Pause/Resume, seek, volume and Audio Mute. Press Escape twice to End session. Audio Mute silences output; Mic Mute stops capture (capture is unavailable here). Quit from the app menu or Ctrl+Q exits the app and its owned backend. Closing the last window also Quits on Linux.

Resources stay inside the app. Preferences/history and unencrypted SQLite data stay in the selected user-data folder. Replacing the executable retains those files.
